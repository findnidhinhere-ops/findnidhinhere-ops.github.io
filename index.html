About Me

I’m Nidhin Chandrasekharan, a Counselling Psychologist and the founder of Good Thoughts Counselling Clinic, an appointment-based counselling practice based in Kerala, India.

With 4+ years of experience in psychology and counselling, I provide professional psychological support for adolescents and adults experiencing emotional, interpersonal and personal challenges.

My work is rooted in empathy, emotional safety, unconditional positive regard and trauma-informed care. I aim to provide a space where you can explore your experiences openly, without judgement, and work towards meaningful personal growth.

My Approach

“Therapy is not about fixing you — it is about understanding you.”

I use an individualised, collaborative and trauma-informed approach. Rather than following a one-size-fits-all model, I work with each person to understand their concerns, strengths, circumstances and goals.

Depending on the person and their needs, sessions may include psychological exploration, structured therapeutic techniques, emotional regulation, self-reflection and practical coping strategies.
