About How I Can Help Approach Sessions

You Don’t Have to Figure It Out Alone

Sometimes life feels overwhelming, confusing, or simply difficult to make sense of. Counselling can offer a safe place to pause, reflect and understand yourself better.

I’m Nidhin Chandrasekharan, a Counselling Psychologist and founder of Good Thoughts Counselling Clinic in Kerala. I work with adolescents and adults and bring an empathetic, collaborative and trauma-informed approach to counselling.

“Therapy is not about fixing you — it is about understanding you.”

A Space Built Around You

Counselling is collaborative. We start with what matters to you, understand your current concerns, and work at a pace that feels appropriate.

My practice is grounded in empathy, emotional safety, unconditional positive regard and structured psychological approaches tailored to the individual.
