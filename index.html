About Me

Professional counselling grounded in empathy, collaboration and psychological understanding.

My work

I’m Nidhin Chandrasekharan, a Counselling Psychologist with experience supporting individuals through personal, emotional, relationship, academic and work-related concerns.

My role is not to tell you what your life should look like. I aim to create a space where you can pause, reflect, understand patterns and work towards goals that matter to you.
